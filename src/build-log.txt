SESSION 1 / PHASE 1 (2026-10-02, Opus): THE DETERMINISTIC CORE IN ENGINE:WORLD. COMPLETE.
  ENGINE:WORLD: var ENGINE_WORLD, deep frozen, version 1.0.0, one global, reads no host global. The render engine is passed
    in as an argument wherever it is needed (climate.table(art, render)), so Day 150 loads engine-world.js beside

    (engine-progression, engine-overworld, engine-interiors, engine-zones, engine-checks, in that order when present).
  Determinism rules (static scan in phase1): no Math.random; no for in; Object.keys only inside util.keys, which sorts;
    no Date or performance; DECISION: no transcendental Math (sin, cos, tan, exp, log, pow, atan, hypot, cbrt and kin),
    because their last bits may differ between browsers and a world must regenerate identically on any device when it is
    not baked. sqrt, floor, abs, imul, and the four basic operations are exact under IEEE 754.
  API: util {keys, each, clamp, digest (FNV-1a over numbers rounded to 1e-9 or strings)}; hash {xmur3, str (first output),
    seed(master, key) = str(master + '|' + key)}; mulberry32; rng(seed) -> fn with int, range (inclusive), float, chance,
    pick, shuffle (Fisher-Yates on a copy), weighted (weights or {w}; index or -1); ids {PREFIXES, slug,
    structural(prefix, key) = prefix + slug(key, 40) + '_' + 4 base36 chars of str(prefix + key), always valid for
    ID_RE, refuses non world prefixes}; noise {simplex(seed) -> {noise2} (Gustavson 2D, permutation shuffled by
    rng(seed), 12 gradients, output in [-1, 1]), fbm(gen, x, y, o), ridge(gen, x, y, o) (octaves of (1 - |n|)^2, in
    [0, 1]), field(w, h, seed, o) -> Float64Array, o {octaves 4, lacunarity 2, gain 0.5, frequency 1, scale 32, ridge}};
    each octave is offset (k * 19.19, k * -7.31) so octaves never line up at the origin; quantize {band(v, th) = number
    of the four cut points v reaches, bands, quantile(arr, fractions) cut points by sorted rank, normalize to [0, 1]};
    climate {index(t, m, e) = t*25 + m*5 + e, inBox, table(art, render), lookup(table, t, m, e)}.
  Climate table: calls render.tiles.matchClimate(tils, t, m, e) for all 125 cells over the biome tilesets in
    render.tiles.prioList(art) order, recording each cell's tileset ID and whether the point lies inside that box.
    Returns {order, cells, exact, exactCount, nearestCount, features [{id, key, feature, temp, moist, elev}], keys, digest}.
    Default biomes: 109 exact, 16 nearest (coast at temperature 0 elevation 1; swamp, forest, grassland at a few wet or hot
    corners). Feature biomes (volcanic) never fill a cell; they are listed for Phase 3's placement pass. Editing a box in
    the art changes the table, and a deleted biome falls back to the nearest box, never to nothing.
  FINDING for Phase 3: at temperature 0 the table gives Snowfield, not Mountains, for elevation 4 (the snow box is tighter
    and wins), and Snowfield is passable (flags 3). Walls, region ridges, and landing beach rings must therefore be placed
    with the mountain biome directly (the tileset whose box is elevation 4 and flags 0), not looked up through the table,
    or a cold ridge would leak. Elevation 0 is Ocean in every column.
  Speed: a 256 by 256 five octave field takes about 150 ms in Node here; the climate table about 2 ms. Phase 3 runs several
    layers, so the Web Worker (importScripts engine-world.js) is likely needed on mobile; measure in Phase 3 before adding.

    table), build(spec), reach(ow, held, opts), walkable, check(ow, graph), grid {bfs, components, largest, nb4}}. spec =

  Palette: ocean from the climate table (elevation 0); walls use the mountain biome directly (box elevation 4, not passable,

    from interior:<kind> with the planned fallbacks (cave and castle to dungeon, town to whichever exists). Stamps: town 3x2

    for the finale with pillars and torches when the set has them. Any missing piece is a palette problem, not a crash.
  Build order: continents (seeded rejection sampling, 120 tries, 6 cell separation, radius sqrt(landFraction x area x minutes
    share / pi) x the continent's radius override) -> masks (1 - d/r + ruggedness x noise, minus an edge falloff over the
    outer 12 cells so coasts curve away from the border) -> strongest mask wins, close contests and the 3 cell margin are
    ocean, and any land within two cells (Chebyshev) of another continent is cut, so straits are at least two cells wide ->
    each continent keeps its largest landmass -> sea is water joined to the edge, the rest are lakes -> elevation: coast is
    land one step from water, or two where noise allows; mountains are the top mountainShare of inland cells (sea distance
    4+) by 0.65 ridge + 0.35 height; high ground the top highShare of the rest -> temperature (latitude, minus height) and
    moisture (distance to water, plus noise) cut into five bands at world.settings.thresholds over the land -> biome by
    climate.lookup -> regions -> rings and ridges -> gates -> feature biomes -> sites and locks -> carving -> walls.
  Regions: one continent per region when alone; a shared continent is cut into bands across a seeded direction, shares by
    chapter minutes, noisy edges, stray pieces joined to the band they touch. Every land cell touching another region's cell
    is a wall (a two cell ridge).
  DECISION, rings: after the ship arrives, every later region that touches the sea is ringed, not only landings. The ring is
    the region's land at sea distance exactly 3 (every four way path from the shore inland crosses it); the strip outside is
    beach. A landing region's ring holds its landing gate (chapter key plus ship); a region entered over a pass that is
    ringed gets a 'seawall' gate (its chapter key), so the ship can never reach a later chapter's inland early, even on a
    continent shared with earlier chapters. Region and ring gate cells stay mountain; the overlay opens them.
  Gates in world.records map_ (overlay): pass (two adjacent ridge cells, consecutive chapters on one continent, needs the
    later chapter's key), landing, seawall, lock. DECISION, the lock: the cell in front of the boss door is a gate needing
    the chapter key and its seal, walled on both sides, ground left as passable lowland so it can be drawn as a seal marker.
  Sites: per region, anchor = the pass's inner cell, else the landing's inner cell, else (chapter one) the cell of the
    largest walkable ground nearest its centroid; anchor and gate sides are carved into the region's largest walkable ground
    first. Candidates need a fully good zone (stamp plus a one cell margin, the front, and for bosses the lock row and
    approach) on inland low or high ground, no feature, no coast or peak, reachable from the anchor; start towns from the
    nearest 20 percent by walking distance, key dungeons 40 to 65, bosses the farthest 15, optional anywhere; spacing from
    siteSpacing (8) down to 3. Summed area table over the region's bounding box. Afterward every site front and lock
    approach is joined to the anchor by carving (shortest path inside the region; blocked cells become passable lowland).
  Features: volcanic (any feature biome) last among the terrain, on inland open cells inside its own box, ranked by its own
    noise, featureCoverage of the eligible cells.
  Self check (overworld.check): for each chapter n, holding what earlier chapters granted (airship left out on purpose, as
    every site entrance already needs its chapter key): every non boss front and each lock's approach reachable, the lock
    shut; with the seal the lock opens; no inner ground of a later region reachable. A build that fails retries with the
    next attempt key (overworld|<name>|<attempt>, attempt 0 has no suffix) up to settings.overworld.attempts (6).
